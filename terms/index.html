The ocean is unpredictable

Swimming, snorkeling, surfing, bodyboarding and even walking near the water in Hawaiʻi can lead to serious injury or death. Surf, currents and weather can change quickly, and conditions at a beach on the day you visit may be very different from what any guide describes. The information on Best Beaches Hawaii is general travel information. It is not a safety assessment of any beach on any day, and it does not replace the instructions of lifeguards, posted signs, park staff or official advisories. When in doubt, stay out of the water.

Lifeguard coverage, parking, fees, reservation rules, closures and laws change over time. Please check current information with the relevant county, state or park authority before you go.
